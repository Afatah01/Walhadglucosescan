Touch your sensor to your phone. Read your glucose.

Walhad Glucose Scan reads a glucose sensor with the phone's own NFC reader, draws the numbers on a clean chart, keeps a real history, and explains what they mean in plain English — including an AI assistant that works only from your own readings.

Three things happen in the first minute

Nothing to configure, nothing to sign up for. Take the phone out and touch the sensor.

Touch the sensor with the phone

The phone's NFC reader wakes the sensor and reads the last eight hours of readings straight from it. No internet needed for this part.

The numbers appear on the screen

Current value, an arrow showing where it is heading, and a chart of the day. All calculated on the phone from the sensor's own data.

Ask what it means, in plain English

An AI assistant answers from your readings only — never invented numbers, never a dose suggestion.

Reading the sensor through the back of the phone

Hold the top of the phone against the round sensor for a second or two. The app reads the sensor's memory over NFC, works out the glucose values, and stores them on the phone. It works with no mobile signal at all.

The app is not made by, and is not affiliated with, any sensor manufacturer. It is a reader for a sensor you already wear.

Everything you would want from a reader

All of it runs on your phone. Your readings stay yours.

Charts and time in range

3, 6, 12 and 24 hour ranges, plus 7, 14, 30 and 90 day statistics: average, time in range, variability, high and low events.

Analysis that adds up

The same figures are used on every screen for the same time window, so Home, Analysis, Insights and Report never disagree.

No doses, no diagnosis

The app never suggests a dose or names a condition. It describes what the numbers show and what is worth raising with your clinician.

Live values, if you want them

Optional: connect over Bluetooth to the sensor itself, or fetch the values from a follower account, once a minute while the app is in the background.

A report you can hand over

Two-page-style PDF report with charts, daily and hourly tables, high and low events, and an AI-written interpretation. Share it or print it.

Alarms and voice

Low, high, and low-coming-soon alarms, plus a voice that can say the value out loud or after a scan.

Where the numbers come from. The app reads the data your FreeStyle Libre sensor holds, either by touching it with the phone's NFC reader or, if you switch it on, from the LibreLinkUp follower service. Everything else — the charts, statistics, report and the AI's explanation — is calculated from those readings on your own phone.

Before you rely on it

Walhad Glucose Scan is a reader and an explainer. It is not a medical device and not a replacement for your sensor maker's own app.

  • Never change insulin or any medicine because of what this app says.
  • If a value looks wrong, confirm it with your sensor maker's app or a fingerstick before acting.
  • Forecasts shown in the app are estimates, not measurements.
  • In an emergency, follow your clinician's plan and contact them.
